Closed Loop Works, but Open Loop Is the One We Need — and the One We’re Building

In a closed loop like stadiums, arenas, campuses, convention centers, we hand you the cup or foodware, and you hand it back before you leave. That's the version that works today, and it's where our 90% return rate comes from.

Open loop is the frontier. It’s reusable takeout from any restaurant in your city and another bin at home, right next to the recycling.

Our infrastructure can deliver open-loop reuse at scale, and it's the version that changes everything.

Several States Now Require What We’ve Been Working On Since 2017

We built nine years ahead of the regulations, but California's SB54 now mandates that 10% of packaged goods be sold in reusable packaging by 2032. Other states have started enacting extended producer responsibility laws with fines for single-use producers.
