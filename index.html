r.World Has Solved for Closed Loop Reuse. Open Loop Reuse Is Next.

In a closed loop like stadiums, arenas, campuses, and convention centers, r.World provides the cup or foodware, and guests return it before leaving. This version works today, driving r.World’s 90% return rate.

Open loop is the frontier. It’s reusable takeout from any restaurant in your city and another bin at home, right next to the recycling.

r.World’s infrastructure and technology can deliver open-loop reuse at scale, and it’s the version changing everything.
