How r.World plans to use the funds

More sanitization centers and deeper automation.

Sanitization centers are what open the market since the network only serves what it can reach and wash. Automation increases reuse's ability to compete with single-use, and cost per item is reduced when each item needs fewer touches.

Research into what single-use can’t do

This enables sponsorship a facility can actually sell against. As Extended Producer Responsibility rules land, with California first in line, reuse could be built for those categories without it, including consumer packaged goods.
