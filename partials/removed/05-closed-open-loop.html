r.World pioneered closed-loop reuse. Now we're building the infrastructure to bring open-loop reuse nationwide.

r.World has built the technology, infrastructure and proven system to make reuse work at scale.

Our current waste system is one way. You use something and it becomes waste. In a closed loop like sports and music venues, r.World provides the cup or foodware, and guests return it before leaving. This version works today, driving r.World's 90% return rate.12

Open-loop reuse is coming, where any container can be returned and reused.

r.World's infrastructure is positioned to process open-loop reuse. We are raising capital to expand markets and gain efficiencies in operations.
